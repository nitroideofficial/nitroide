Common Questions

Straight answers before you switch.

Is NitroIDE really free, unlike Replit's credit system?

Yes. NitroIDE runs 100% in your browser, so there are no servers to meter and no credits to burn. Every feature is unlocked at $0, forever.

How do I move my Replit project to NitroIDE?

Copy your files into a public GitHub Gist, then open nitroide.com/tools/codebox.html?gist=YOUR_GIST_ID. Our step-by-step migration tutorial walks through the whole move.

Can NitroIDE run my Replit backend code?

Honestly: no. NitroIDE is a frontend workspace (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) — it doesn't run Python, Node servers or databases. If your project is pure frontend, you'll feel right at home; if you need a backend, Replit is still the better fit.
