Privacy & License

TL;DR: Your code runs only in your browser — NitroIDE never sees it. NitroIDE does operate a small backend, but only for contact/feedback forms, the newsletter, analytics, and ads, each disclosed below. We do not track your keystrokes and we do not sell your data.

1. Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026. Controller: NitroIDE, operated by Yash Panchal. Contact: contactnitroide@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The IDE execution engine runs entirely in your browser (HTML5 srcdoc iframes and client-side JavaScript). The code you write, the files in the Virtual File System (VFS), and settings such as theme preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to NitroIDE.

Deleting local data: clearing your browser's cache or local storage permanently deletes unsaved workspaces. Use the Export features in the IDE to save your work to your hard drive.

What leaves your device (and why)

Contact & feedback forms. When you submit the contact form or the in-site feedback form, your name, email address, message, and any attachments you add are sent to a Cloudflare Worker that forwards them by email (via Resend) to the operator so your message can be answered. Your details are used only to reply to your message. Form emails are kept only as long as needed to respond and are deleted on request.

Newsletter. If you subscribe, your email address is sent to Buttondown, which delivers new-article notifications. You can unsubscribe at any time from any newsletter email.

AI features (bring your own key). NitroIDE has no AI backend of its own. If you use the AI pair programmer, the code you choose to send goes directly to the AI provider you selected (Groq, Gemini, OpenRouter, or OpenAI) using the API key you entered. The one exception is SambaNova, which passes through a non-storing Cloudflare Worker proxy because the provider cannot be called directly from the browser. Your API keys are stored only in your browser, never on NitroIDE servers. Your use of each provider is governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy.

Share links. The Share button compresses your workspace into the link itself (?code=). Nothing is uploaded to NitroIDE — but anyone who receives the link can open and read the code it contains, so share links only with people you trust.

CDN injector. If you use the CDN injector to fetch external libraries (like React or Tailwind), your browser makes standard network requests to those CDN providers (e.g., unpkg, cdnjs). Those requests are governed by the respective providers' privacy policies.

Deploy. Deploying to GitHub Pages or Netlify uses your own token and your own account; NitroIDE never sees your credentials beyond passing them to the service you chose.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to measure aggregate site usage (page views and sessions) so we can improve NitroIDE. Analytics loads only after you grant consent in the cookie banner, and you can withdraw consent at any time via the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. Analytics never sees your code: the page address sent to Google is reduced to the page path plus marketing (utm_*) parameters only — share-link contents, API keys, and everything else in the URL are stripped before any beacon is sent. GA4 event-data retention is 2 months. You can also opt out via Google's Analytics opt-out add-on; see also how Google uses data from partner sites.

Advertising & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads that keep NitroIDE free. AdSense loads only after you grant consent. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info. For more information on how Google uses data, see Google's partner-sites policy.

Sub-processors

We rely on the following service providers, each only for the purpose listed: Google (Analytics and AdSense), Cloudflare (hosting, content delivery, and the form/AI-proxy Workers), Resend (form email delivery), and Buttondown (newsletter delivery).

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you (such as a form message or a newsletter subscription), to receive a portable copy, or to withdraw consent. Email contactnitroide@gmail.com and we will respond to reasonable requests. Local data in your browser (code, workspaces, settings) is under your control — clearing browser storage deletes it.

Do Not Sell: NitroIDE does not sell your personal information.

Children: NitroIDE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. There are no accounts and no age-gated signups.

Retention summary: form messages — only as long as needed to respond (deleted on request); newsletter — until you unsubscribe; analytics — GA4 event data retained 2 months; local browser data — until you clear it.

Changes: if this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above and note the change in the changelog.
